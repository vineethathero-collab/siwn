Our Services

Key social, spiritual, and educational services conducted by Siwmaga Dharmayathanaya

Association with the Maha Sangha...

Gaining noble spiritual friendship (Kalyanamittata) through association with the Supreme Maha Sangha.

Dhamma Knowledge & Understanding...

Realizing the Four Noble Truths and gaining deep Dhamma wisdom through noble guidance.

Virtues, Values & Social Ethics...

Instilling virtuous conduct and wholesome character modeled after ancient Arahants and noble disciples.

Buddhist Traditions & Cultural Values...

Preserving and imparting cherished ancient Sinhalese Buddhist customs and moral heritage to children.

Wisdom from Sacred Texts...

Presenting timeless wisdom from sacred texts such as the Dhammapada, Jataka stories, and Loweda Sangarawa in an engaging manner for young minds.

Child Skill & Personality Development...

Creating an encouraging foundation for children to utilize time meaningfully and develop exemplary character.
